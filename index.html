Research Identity

Research Focus & PhD Interests

My research sits at the intersection of nutritional science, biochemistry, and population health — examining how dietary exposures translate into measurable metabolic and immunological outcomes in resource-constrained settings.

🫀

Diet–Cardiometabolic Pathways

Examining how dietary quality and patterns — derived via PCA — associate with hypertension, body fat percentage, and metabolic syndrome components in rural Bangladeshi adults.

🧬

Nutritional Biomarkers

Profiling serum immunoglobulins, liver function enzymes, and body composition indices as nutritional and metabolic health markers in population-based cohorts.

📊

Epidemiological Methods

Principal component analysis for dietary pattern derivation; multivariate logistic and linear regression; dietary assessment validation; body composition measurement comparisons.

🌏

LMICs & South Asian Health

Generating locally-grounded evidence on the nutrition transition in Bangladesh — where undernutrition and overnutrition coexist — to inform context-sensitive public health strategies.

PhD Research Interests

I am seeking doctoral opportunities in nutritional epidemiology, public health nutrition, or diet–disease research with a focus on cardiometabolic outcomes in low- and middle-income country populations. I am particularly interested in labs working on dietary assessment methodology, metabolic syndrome in South Asian contexts, or the application of advanced statistical models (multilevel, mediation, time-series) to nutrition data.
